Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

SHIELD is a pharmacy app run by Zabnix Private Limited ("SHIELD", "we", "us"). This page explains what information we collect when you use the SHIELD app, why we collect it, and who we share it with. We collect only what the app needs to verify your account, deliver your order, and rank the branch nearest you — nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to us at zabnixprivatelimited@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhen
Name and mobile number When you create an account. Sign-in itself is a one-time code (OTP) sent to your number by Firebase Phone Authentication (a Google service) — we never see or store the code itself, only that your number was verified.
Device location (GPS) Only if you tap "use current location" to find the nearest SHIELD branch or speed up address entry. Entirely optional — registration and checkout work the same way with just a pincode if you decline or your device can't get a fix.
Delivery address Pincode, house/flat, area and landmark you type in, or that get filled in from a location fix you shared.
Orders and prescription images When you place an order or upload a prescription (from your camera or gallery) so our pharmacists can fulfil it.
Rewards / wallet balance Tracked against your account as you earn or redeem points.

2. How we use it

  • Verify it's really you signing in (OTP via Firebase).
  • Rank SHIELD branches by distance and pre-fill your address.
  • Prepare, dispense and deliver your order.
  • Keep your order history, rewards and wallet balance up to date.
  • Respond if you contact support.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not run any ad or analytics tracking SDKs in the app.

3. Who we share it with

  • Firebase (Google) — sends the OTP and confirms your number for sign-in.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — if you share your GPS location, the coordinates are sent to this open, no-key mapping service to turn them into a pincode/area name. No account information is sent with it.
  • Our database provider (Neon) — hosts the Postgres database your account, orders and address records live in, as our infrastructure provider.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing purposes.

4. How long we keep it

Account, order and prescription records are kept for as long as your account is active, so your order history and repeat prescriptions stay available to you. Ask us to delete your account (see below) and we will remove your personal data except where we're required to keep order/prescription records for pharmacy or tax record-keeping.

5. Your choices

  • Location sharing is opt-in every time — decline it and a pincode still gets you a nearby branch.
  • You can sign out of the app at any time from the account menu.
  • To have your account and personal data deleted, email zabnixprivatelimited@gmail.com from the number on your account.

6. Security

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Phone Authentication rather than a password we'd have to store. Data in transit to our servers is sent over HTTPS.

7. Children

SHIELD is not directed at children, and account creation requires a working personal mobile number to receive an OTP.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who we share it with, we'll update the effective date above.
